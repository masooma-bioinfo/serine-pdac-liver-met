Question: Are serine-synthesis genes (PHGDH, PSAT1, PSPH) different between primary pancreatic tumors and their matched liver metastases?
Data: GSE151580, bulk RNA-seq, 13 patients with both samples.
Method: counts per million, log2, paired Wilcoxon test, Bonferroni correction for 3 genes.
Result: PSPH was lower in the liver metastasis in 11 of 13 patients (corrected p = 0.04). PSAT1 and PHGDH showed no reliable difference after correction.
Limits: small sample; liver cells mixed into metastasis samples; the 6 normal samples are pancreas, so they cannot control for this; exploratory only.
